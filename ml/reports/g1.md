# Gate G1

The encoder is adopted. The serving manifest is `v1.0.0-38ecb9bd` with
`encoder_enabled` true. The graph is the final fit, not the laptop smoke run.

Accuracy passed: fusion Stage A macro-F1 0.802 versus `svc_word_char` 0.656.

Latency was measured with:

```text
docker run --rm --cpus 2 --memory 4g --memory-swap 4g --network none
```

The cap is 2 CPUs and 4 GB with no swap. The cores are this laptop's, not the
Azure B2als_v2. ONNX Runtime was limited to one intra-op thread.

| slice | n | p50 ms | p95 ms | max ms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| validation sample | 50 | 17.8 | 36.1 | 48.8 |
| 10k-character text | 20 | 64.9 | 73.2 | 75.3 |

Resident memory after the run was 499 MB. Five thousand long tickets at that p95
are about 6 minutes, inside the 30 minute job limit. Section 5.4 asked for an
encoder around 60-150 ms and a resident set under about 2 GB.

The serving manifest now has `encoder_enabled` true. The graph is the final fit
(4 epochs on all 4,800 tickets, batch 8 with 2 accumulation steps), not the smoke
checkpoint. Dynamic int8 is the file in the image. On 200 validation tickets,
fused labels match the fp32 graph exactly even though raw int8 probabilities
can move by about 0.19.
